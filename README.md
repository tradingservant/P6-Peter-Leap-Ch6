# P6-Peter-Leap-Ch6

Peter · Reading & Writing Chapter 6. A colourful, tap-friendly practice game for Primary 6 English.

Theme: **Fun with films.** Motto: **What kinds of films do you like?**

Vocabulary and grammar questions are Traditional Chinese. Vocabulary answers stay a picture plus the English phrase. The pictures are cropped from the book page. The three film reviews, the Film Club newsletter, and the model film review stay in English. Buttons, help, and feedback are Traditional Chinese.

This chapter’s writing task is a **film review**. It is the unit task, Your Task: Writing a film review. Chapter 5 *who / which / if I were* is not taught here. Those words may appear only as linking words in the film-review station, because the book lists them.

There is no listening. The soft *g* / hard *g* task is a visual sort, then a finish-the-rule question.

## What is inside

1. **詞彙 Vocabulary** — eight phrasal verbs from the book pictures: turn into, find out, get away, get into (trouble), give up, look for, make up (a story), set off (for a trip). *make up with*, *make up your mind*, and *make-up* are recognition only.
2. **文法 Grammar** — *-ed* for feelings and *-ing* for the film, plot, or special effects. Members’ Corner uses each word once.
3. **閱讀 Reviews** — *Kung Fu Panda 3*, *Ice Age: Collision Course*, and *Captain America: Civil War*. Then a visual soft *g* / hard *g* sort.
4. **閱讀 Newsletter** — Film Club cloze for *Smurfs: The Lost Village* and *The Secret Life of Pets*.
5. **寫作 Film review** — order the three parts, choose the tense, finish *The LEGO Batman Movie*, then write a review of about 80–120 words.

A wrong try says 再試一次. The next wrong try shows a hint, and the pupil tries again. On a gap-fill, the third wrong try fills that gap so a long cloze can still finish. The first correct try, before any miss, is the one that counts as perfect.

## Answer key

Correct answers follow the Chapter 6 spec. Model answers are marked 範例／教師用.

- Picture match is A turn into through H set off.
- *Make up your mind* means make a decision. *Make up with someone* means become friendly again.
- Members’ Corner: amusing, moving, boring, disappointed, amused, moved, surprised, amazing, amazed, disappointing.
- Dialogue lines: (i) C, (ii) A, (iii) B, (iv) A. The true sentence is that each film is about characters from earlier films.
- Soft *g* (giant, page, gym) is followed by e, i or y. Hard *g* (gate, good, guess) is followed by a, o or u.
- Newsletter forms include finds out, makes up, and turn Smurfette into. The same base verb can be used again.
- A film review goes summary (present), comments (past), message (present).

The open film review is for the teacher. The checklist is a guide, not the only acceptable answer.
